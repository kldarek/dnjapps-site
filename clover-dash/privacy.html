Who provides the game

Clover Dash is provided by Dariusz Kleczek under the DNJ Apps name. Privacy contact: learnbychatai@gmail.com.

Your game data

The app does not require an account or send your gameplay, profile, device identifiers or location to us. Your outfits, stars, gems, settings and record are stored on your device. There is no cloud save. Deleting the app may permanently erase this data.

No advertising or developer analytics

The initial App Store release has no ads or developer-operated analytics. Its bundled ad integration is disabled before initialization. Earlier TestFlight builds with demo ads are outside this public-release policy. Apple may process App Store and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings, and may provide developers with reports.

Optional sharing

Sharing creates a temporary image of your result, horse and rider and opens the system share sheet. You decide where to send it; the app does not upload it automatically. The temporary file is removed after sharing or cancellation.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to send through Gmail. We use this only to help with your request, not for marketing. We delete correspondence when no longer needed, normally within 12 months of resolution unless law requires longer retention. You may request earlier deletion.

This website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting. We add no analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or contact forms. See our website privacy information.

Your rights

Support and website operation rely on our legitimate interests in responding to requests and maintaining the service where applicable. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or object to processing. Contact us at the address above. You may complain to your local data protection authority, including Poland’s President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). Providers may process data internationally under their applicable safeguards.

Children and updates

The app does not ask children for personal information. A parent or guardian can contact us about information sent to support. We will update this policy before introducing changes to our data practices.
