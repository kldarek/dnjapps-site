About this policy

This policy covers Clover Dash, published under the DNJAPPS brand. For privacy questions and requests, contact learnbychatai@gmail.com.

Clover Dash uses Google AdMob in versions where advertising is enabled. The advertising sections below apply whenever ads are enabled; availability can differ by app version, region, connection and privacy choices. An ad-free version does not request ads. This policy is information about data processing, not consent to advertising or tracking.

Game progress and sharing

No account is required. Your game progress, outfits, stars, gems, settings and records are stored on your device. DNJAPPS does not operate a cloud-save service or receive a copy of this local save. Deleting the app or losing the device may erase it.

Optional sharing creates a temporary result image and opens the system share sheet. You choose the recipient or app; the game does not upload the image automatically. The temporary file is removed after sharing or cancellation. The service you choose handles the shared image under its own rules.

Google AdMob

When ads are enabled, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it; device or app identifiers; ad impressions, views and interactions; app interactions; crash reports, performance and other diagnostic data. This supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service reliability. Approximate location does not require access to precise GPS location.

Ad data is transmitted to Google and, where an ad involves them, advertising partners. DNJAPPS can receive advertising reports. Google determines some processing for its own services; the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps explain this processing and available controls. An absence of a name or account does not make all advertising data anonymous.

Privacy choices and children

The game’s ad integration is configured for child-directed treatment and non-personalized advertising. This restricts interest-based advertising; it does not mean that no technical data is processed. DNJAPPS does not ask children to supply personal details. A parent or guardian should handle support requests for a child and can contact learnbychatai@gmail.com about deletion.

Where consent is required for advertising-related processing or storage/access on your device, it must be obtained before that activity. Any consent message explains the available choices and how to revisit them. Where shown, use the in-app privacy options to change or withdraw consent. You may also contact us for help. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. Apple’s tracking permission, if requested in an applicable version, is separate; it can be managed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Non-personalized ads may still require consent under local rules.

Support, website and Apple

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments through Gmail. Providing this information is optional, but we need enough detail to answer. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. We use support messages to respond and resolve problems, not to build marketing lists.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request data, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure it. DNJAPPS adds no website analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or forms. See website privacy. Apple separately processes App Store and diagnostic data according to its policies and your settings, and may provide reports to developers.

Legal bases, retention and transfers

Where the GDPR applies, support and basic service security rely on legitimate interests in answering requests and protecting the service; legal obligations apply where records must be kept by law. Consent applies where required for advertising or device access. These interests do not override a consent requirement.

Local saves remain until removed or replaced on the device. Support correspondence is deleted when no longer needed, normally within 12 months of resolution, unless a legal obligation or a specific dispute requires longer retention. Google and GitHub retain service data under their published policies; DNJAPPS does not set a single retention period for all provider data.

Google, GitHub and their service providers may process data outside Poland and the EEA. Their privacy policies describe applicable transfer arrangements, such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses, and how to request more information about safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. Contact learnbychatai@gmail.com; we may request proportionate information to verify your request. We cannot retrieve a save held only on your device. For data independently controlled by a provider, its privacy controls and request channels also apply.

You may complain to a data protection authority, including Poland’s President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). DNJAPPS does not use your data to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Policy changes

We update this notice when data practices change and show the revision date above. Material changes will be communicated as required. New consent will be requested where required; continuing to use the game is not a substitute for consent.
