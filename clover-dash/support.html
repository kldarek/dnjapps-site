Contact

For help with Clover Dash, email learnbychatai@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

How to play

Hold your device sideways. Tap for a short jump, or hold for a higher, longer jump. Release before jumping again. Use the pause button to stop a ride and the stable to change your horse and rider.

Progress and offline play

The iPhone and iPad app works offline. Progress, outfits and settings stay on your device; there is no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app can permanently remove your progress.

Troubleshooting

Close and reopen the app, then check for updates. Avoid deleting it while troubleshooting. If the issue continues, contact us.

The initial App Store release is free, with no advertising or in-app purchases.
